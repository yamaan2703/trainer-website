# Content Notes & Issues Found

Observations made while extracting the copy. These are not site content — they are flagged for whoever maintains the site.

## Live copy problems

1. **Footer copyright reads "© 0000"** — on every page. The Webflow dynamic-year script is not populating, so the footer literally shows `© 0000 Cameron Clark Fitness.`

2. **Truncated testimonial on `/service/in-person-training`** — Jake S.'s quote ends mid-sentence: *"…tailors his approach in a very"*. The same quote appears complete in the Home page slider: *"…tailors his approach in a very understanding way."* The In-Person version is missing the final two words.

3. **Unreachable pricing tab on `/service/online-coaching-program`** — the pricing block is a two-pane Webflow tab component with no tab links rendered. The second pane ("Annual Savings" $1,800 / "Business Plan" $3,200, both linking to `#`) is in the HTML but cannot be reached by a visitor. The wording ("All program features included", "Exclusive member resources", "Community support access") reads as unedited Relume/Webflow template placeholder text, and both prices contradict the visible pricing.

4. **Duplicate "Save 20% with the annual plan" label** — used under both hidden plans, including the $3,200 Business Plan where no annual comparison is shown.

5. **Meta description missing** on `/contact-thank-you` and `/discovery-call-thank-you`.

6. **Inconsistent meta titles on legal pages** — `/privacy-policy` and `/terms-and-conditions` use bare titles ("Privacy Policy", "Terms and Conditions") with no brand suffix, unlike every other page.

## Structural observations

7. **Boilerplate privacy/terms** — both legal pages are the standard Shopify-derived e-commerce template, referring throughout to "our store", purchases, and products. The site sells coaching services, not store merchandise, and has no storefront. Governing law is given only as "the laws of USA" with no state specified.

8. **A testimonial is reused across pages** — Jake S. appears on both Home and In-Person Training, in two different lengths (see #2).

9. **Forms are entirely third-party** — both the contact form and the discovery-call booking calendar are LeadConnector / HighLevel iframes. None of their labels, placeholders, validation messages or button text live in this site's markup, so none of it is editable in Webflow and none is captured above.

10. **Testimonial markup pattern** — each testimonial is a lead-in `<p>` followed by an emphasised `<h2>` and an attribution `<p>`. The `<h2>` is used for visual emphasis rather than document structure, which means testimonial fragments compete with real section headings in the page's heading outline.

11. **Home page heading order** — the process steps ("Discovery Call", "Strategic Onboarding", "Execute & Optimize") are marked up as `<h2>`, at the same level as the section heading that introduces them.
